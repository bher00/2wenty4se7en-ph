2wenty4se7en

2wenty4se7en is a private office tool used by one small team. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

The team's closers record their calls in a Google Sheet. 2wenty4se7en watches that sheet for changes, such as a call's status changing, and shows them to the team. When a team member approves a change, the tool copies it into the team's own status sheet in Google Sheets.

Google access

The tool signs in to Google with the team's own account and uses it to:

  • read the closers' call sheet, which is shared with that account,
  • write approved changes to the team's status sheet, and
  • be told by Google Drive when the call sheet changes, so changes show as soon as they're made.

It doesn't read any other files, email or account data.
